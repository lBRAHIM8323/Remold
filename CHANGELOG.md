# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- **Convert to ▸** in the Explorer and editor tab menus, with the common targets for each kind of file, and
  **More Formats…** with everything [Filee](https://filee.sh) converts the file to (300+ formats).
- Conversions by Filee: your own Filee 2.5 or later, or a copy Remold downloads once (after asking) from Filee's
  official releases, checks against Filee's signature, keeps up to date within 2.x, and removes when Remold is
  uninstalled.
- Sizes of the suggested formats before converting (images and vector graphics).
- **Update References** after converting, shown in VS Code's Refactor Preview.
- **Insert as WEBP (Remold)** when dropping or pasting images into Markdown and HTML.
- **Install Filee Engines…** for conversions that need an optional engine (Calibre, Pandoc, FFmpeg, …).
